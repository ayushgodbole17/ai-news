01  ·  speech  ·  arXiv
IndicFDB: Benchmarking Full-Duplex Voice Agents across Indian Languages

A new benchmark for full-duplex voice agents (handling pauses, turn-taking, backchannel and interruptions) built specifically for Indian languages, addressing the English-only corpus and English-prompted judge limits of the existing Full-Duplex-Bench.

02  ·  eval  ·  arXiv
mu-bench: A Multilingual Utterance Transcription Benchmark

A dataset of 4,270 call-center utterances for evaluating ASR used by voice agents, arguing that standard word-error-rate scoring on read, English-centric speech penalizes surface differences rather than the semantic errors that actually matter for acting on what a caller said.

Why you care: Call-center ASR evaluation is exactly your call-analytics problem, and the critique of WER as a metric bears directly on how you should be scoring transcription quality.

03  ·  speech  ·  arXiv
How to Reduce Whisper Hallucination

An analysis of Whisper hallucination showing that on 42 clips of pure room tone, whisper-large-v3 still emits words 61.9% of the time and emits something on 100% of clips, with proposed ways to cut this down.

Why you care: Whisper-class hallucination directly affects ASR quality and downstream transcript reliability in your voice-agent and call-analytics pipelines.

04  ·  eval  ·  arXiv
CoSE-E: A Benchmark for Code-switched Speech Evaluation in Enterprise Settings

A benchmark for code-switched speech recognition in enterprise contexts, arguing existing code-switching ASR work focuses on conversational speech and doesn't evaluate operational impact beyond edit-distance metrics.

Why you care: Code-mixed speech quality is a constant problem you name explicitly for Hindi/Tamil ASR, and this targets exactly the enterprise-call setting your work sits in.

05  ·  safety  ·  arXiv
API Secrets Should Never Become Tokens in the LLM's Vocabulary

A threat analysis of API credential handling in LLM agent systems, arguing that a key pasted into a prompt or system prompt crosses from an authentication boundary into a data pipeline, plus an empirical evaluation of a vault-mediated execution boundary that keeps secrets out of the model's context.

Why you care: This is a concrete architecture question for the governance proxy you're building in front of agents, where credential handling is one of the things a guardrail layer needs to get right.
