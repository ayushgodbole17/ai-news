MultiPartyTurnBench: benchmarking floor control in multi-party spoken dialogue

A benchmark for full-duplex speech systems deciding when to enter, hold or relinquish the conversational floor amid overlapping speakers and backchannels, addressing a gap in evaluations that only study dyadic turn-taking or end-to-end agent behavior.

04  ·  safety  ·  arXiv
TRACER: trace-regularized training against indirect prompt injection

Shows that defenses against indirect prompt injection are channel-conditioned: the same attack succeeds 18% of the time as a follow-up message but 53% when placed in retrieved content, and proposes trace-regularized training to close that gap.

Why you care: Directly relevant to the jailbreak and prompt-injection detection layer of their agent-governance proxy.

Why you care: A negative result worth knowing before adopting feature distillation for compact multilingual ASR, which touches their Hindi/Tamil/Arabic ASR quality problem.
