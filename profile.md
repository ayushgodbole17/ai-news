I'm an AI engineer at a startup. My work moves between projects week to week, so I want a
broad, useful picture of the field, not a feed tuned to one stack or one product.

Right now that spans: AI governance (guardrails, policy enforcement, audit trails,
jailbreak and PII detection, a proxy in front of agents), backend engineering for AI
systems (APIs, queues, databases, reliability, observability; Python/FastAPI,
Node/TypeScript, MongoDB, Docker on VMs), voice and speech AI (real-time voice agents,
speech recognition and synthesis, Indian languages), LLM pipelines (mostly Gemini, some
Anthropic and Groq; I track cost and latency), RAG, evals and LLM-as-judge, and agentic
coding tools.

What I want: the most important new models, tools and releases across AI, with real
substance (features, benchmarks, pricing). I want genuinely new kinds of things the
first time they appear, so I hear about them early. Follow-ups, clones and look-alikes of
something already covered are not news unless they change something big. Agentic coding
tools (Claude Code, plugins, MCP) only when notable. The industry side matters too:
funding, executive moves, policy and regulation (especially AI governance and safety),
and commentary worth reading.

I do not care about: routine version bumps, outage chatter, listicles and "top N tools"
roundups, or vendor case studies.
